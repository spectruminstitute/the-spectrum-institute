About The Spectrum Institute

TSI is a professional education hub in Barikot & Mingora, Swat, focused on industry-ready safety, computer, coaching, and language programs.

Guided by experienced leadership across both campuses.

Branch Manager — Mingora Campus

Amir Hamza

Oversees Mingora City Campus operations at Opposite to Mela Ground, Mingora, Swat.

Our Vision

To make globally relevant technical and safety education accessible in Swat — so students can build careers locally and compete internationally.

We combine practical labs, expert mentorship, and certification-focused pathways across HSE, IT, AI, and academic coaching.

Our Story

Founded to close the skills gap in Barikot and Mingora, TSI grew from a focused training desk into a multi-track institute covering Safety Engineering, Computer Courses, Matric/FSc coaching, and language programs.

Today we serve students across Swat with scholarship support on selected ICT, Cyber Security, AI, and coaching tracks.

Frequently Asked Questions
Are these professional courses certified internationally? +
Yes! Courses like NEBOSH, IOSH, OSHA, and OTHM carry worldwide global accreditations. Our technical IT courses are mapped with global standard competencies perfect for local and international markets.

TSI operates two campuses: Barikot Main Campus (Barikot, Swat) and Mingora City Campus (Opposite to Mela Ground, Mingora, Swat), managed by Branch Manager Amir Hamza. See details and the map on our Contact page.
