About Shah Mujeeb ur Rahman

Shah Mujeeb ur Rahman works across Artificial Intelligence, cyber security and software development. He trains and fine-tunes AI models, builds AI agents (including Pashto-language AI agent systems) and designs complete web and desktop software.

As an AI vulnerability researcher he studies how AI models can be manipulated, and was recognised and rewarded $500 for identifying critical AI model vulnerabilities and manipulation risks.

He developed The Spectrum Institute’s website and its certificate system, in which every certificate can be verified online, and leads IT & AI teaching at TSI, bringing modern technology to students across Swat and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
