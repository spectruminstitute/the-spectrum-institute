About Shah Mujeeb Ur Rahman

Shah Mujeeb Ur Rahman is an AI specialist who works and teaches in the Swat valley. Based in Barikot, Swat, he is the Lead IT & Artificial Intelligence Specialist at The Spectrum Institute (TSI), which trains students from across Swat at its campuses in Barikot and Mingora.

His work covers deep learning, machine learning and computer vision, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines, and fine-tuning large language models, including his custom model work under the name Markhor AI. He also builds AI agent systems for the Pashto language, so that people in Swat and across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa can use AI in their own language.

Alongside building AI, Shah Mujeeb Ur Rahman is an active AI security researcher. He was recognised and rewarded $500 for identifying a critical AI model vulnerability and manipulation risk.

At TSI he teaches AI Engineering, Data Science, Python, Cyber Security and networking. So far he has trained more than 350 students in artificial intelligence: around 200 female students and more than 150 male students from Swat. That makes him one of the people opening up AI careers to young women in Swat, who can now learn AI close to home in Swat.
