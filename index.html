The Spectrum Institute · Swat, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Swat's AI Engineering & IT Academy

The Spectrum Institute (TSI) is where Swat learns Artificial Intelligence. Students from every part of Swat study AI Engineering, Python, Data Science, Cyber Security, Web & Software Development and CCNA networking with practising AI specialists, building real projects instead of just reading slides. They learn at our campuses in Barikot and Mingora, without leaving the valley. We also run internationally recognised Safety Engineering courses (NEBOSH, IOSH, OSHA). Every graduate leaves with a certificate anyone can verify online.

  • ✓ AI courses taught by an AI researcher, not just a trainer
  • ✓ Students from all over Swat: Mingora, Saidu Sharif, Barikot, Kabal, Matta, Khwazakhela, Charbagh, Madyan, Bahrain and Kalam
  • ✓ Two campuses in Barikot & Mingora, taught by expert faculty leads
  • ✓ QR-verified certificates, checked instantly on our Verify page
