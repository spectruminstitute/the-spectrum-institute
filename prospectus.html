01 · Institute Overview

Practical skills, real projects, verified results

The Spectrum Institute (TSI) is Swat’s leading institution for cutting-edge Artificial Intelligence, Advanced IT and internationally recognized Safety Engineering.

We empower students across Swat, from Barikot and Mingora to all surrounding valleys, with hands-on practical skills: building real-world projects rather than just reading slides. All programs carry QR-verified certificates that anyone can check instantly online.

  • AI & Advanced ITAI Engineering, Python, Cyber Security, Web, Networking
  • Safety EngineeringNEBOSH, IOSH, OSHA, ISO, OTHM and NVQ
  • Academic CoachingMatric and FSc science subjects
  • Language CenterEnglish, Japanese, Korean and Chinese

04 · Institute Rules & Code of Conduct

What we expect from every student

  • Punctuality

    Students must arrive on time for all physical classes and computer lab sessions.

  • Discipline & Mobile Phones

    Mobile phones must remain switched off or on silent during lectures and lab hours.

  • Fee Policy

    Monthly or course tuition fees must be cleared by the stipulated due date to ensure uninterrupted enrollment.

  • Equipment Care

    Students are personally responsible for the careful handling of computer systems and laboratory equipment.

  • Certificate Verification

    Every certificate issued by TSI carries an official QR code for instant online verification.
