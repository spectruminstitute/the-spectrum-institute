Why students in Swat choose TSI for AI

Taught by an AI researcher

Lead instructor Shah Mujeeb Ur Rahman, an AI expert from Barikot, works on machine learning, computer vision, RAG systems and LLM fine-tuning (Markhor AI), was rewarded for finding a critical AI model vulnerability, and has trained 350+ students in AI, around 200 of them female.

Projects, not just theory

Students build real things, like AI agents, full-stack web apps and data projects, that they can show employers and clients. Our AI lead has built Pashto-language AI agent systems himself.

AI for all of Swat

Students come to TSI from all over Swat, including Mingora, Saidu Sharif, Barikot, Kabal, Matta, Khwazakhela, Charbagh, Madyan, Bahrain and Kalam, to learn modern AI without moving to Peshawar or Islamabad. Our campuses are in Barikot and Mingora.

Questions about learning AI in Swat

Which is the best AI academy in Swat?

The Spectrum Institute (TSI) is Swat's dedicated AI Engineering and IT academy. AI courses are taught by Shah Mujeeb Ur Rahman, an AI specialist and AI security researcher who has trained 350+ students in AI, through hands-on projects, and every certificate can be verified online.

Where can I learn Artificial Intelligence in Swat?

At The Spectrum Institute, which serves students from across Swat, including Mingora, Saidu Sharif, Barikot, Kabal, Matta, Khwazakhela, Charbagh, Madyan, Bahrain and Kalam. The main campus is on the 1st Floor, Usman Plaza, Main Pull, Barikot, and the city campus is opposite Mela Ground, Mingora. Both are open Monday to Saturday.

I have never coded before. Can I still learn AI?

Yes. Beginners usually start with the Python course, then move on to AI Engineering or Data Science. Our IT desk can suggest the right starting point for you.

Which AI and IT courses does TSI offer?

AI Engineering, Data Science, Python, Cyber Security, Web & Software Development, CCNA / CCNA Security, Database Engineering, C/C++, DIT/CIT, ICT, Graphics Designing, Digital Marketing, E-commerce and Microsoft Office.

Are TSI certificates real and verifiable?

Yes. Every TSI certificate has a QR code and ID that anyone, including employers, can check on our Verify page at spectruminstitute.uk/verify.html.
